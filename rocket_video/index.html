Rocketry Club Story

2026

Short story about a workshop I hosted at Macalester College where the High Power Rocketry Club hosted local girls scouts troop on campus to teach them the basics of rocketry and get them excited about science. In hindsight, I learned more from them than I thought was possible.
I re-started the High Power Rocketry Club at Macalester after many years of it being dormant, and have served as the president for the past three years. In addition to working with girl scouts multiple times, each year we host similar workshops with local elementary schools.
