"Why" Team IMPACT? Video 2026 A story of my inspiration to work with Team IMPACT and spread the beauty of this program to children facing serious illness or disability across the world. The photo is from the 2025 annual Team IMPACT conference where Macalester College was recognized as the best Division III fellowship campus of the year.

This video was a part of my application for the Macalester LiveIT fund, which I applied for to cover my expenses while I worked in Japan with the non-profit Being ALIVE Japan (BAJ). BAJ is similar to Team IMPACT, and I am incredibly grateful to have had this experience and work with another Macalester student (Sarah Solomon) where we ran workshops in hospitals across the island, were guest speakers at 10 different seminars or business meetings, mentored Japanese college students working with BAJ, and dove head first into various community based support initiatives while considering the strong cultural differences that influence the structure of those systems.

Although I was not accepted for this scholarship, I pursued other grants and emailed 100+ alumni to cover the necessary expenses for my 2 months of work.

I was interviewed for and then featured in this article about our work with Team IMPACT at Macalester.
