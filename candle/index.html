Candle Holder

Ceramics
2024

A handbuilt piece that holds a candle in the center section. In this project, all students in the ceramics class made 5 small handbuilt "1-shape" pieces. Then, we put all of them on the table and we each picked 5 new of these shapes to combine together into new pieces. This is one of my combined pieces from that class project.

Included are also some of my initial brainstorming from my ceramics notebook for these pieces.
