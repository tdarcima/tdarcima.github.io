Untitled Ceramics 2024 This is the largest handbuilt piece that I had made at the time. It was inspired by visits to the Minneapolis Institute of Art, where I was drawn to vases of similar form and Japanese interior design shelving with a deep red color sectioned off by intense black geometric patterns. In my piece, the sides of the pattern drape over the side of the vase and the seemingly organized pattern begins to crack. I enjoy playing with an unexpected in color, texture, and form.

Included are also some of my initial brainstorming from my ceramics notebook for this piece.
