How it Goes Down Graphite 2025 Large-form study of hands in different positions from the fall semester of my junior year of college. A year later, I incorporated new techniques I learned while studying abroad to combine this piece with abstract ink paintings.

This drawing depicts the physical process elicited from Misophonia that I have experienced, especially when I was younger, where I feel the stress down in my hands and quickly contract my fingers into a fist. The gradual yet chaotic change of color in the ink also reflects this change in emotion as the fist closes.
