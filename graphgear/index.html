2020 and 2026

In my sophomore year of high school, I took apart my mechanical pencil and made a technical drawing of it as one of my first-ever engineering class assignments. This was basically just an online drawing, and is the image with all red lines.

In the summer before my senior year of college, after working on countless CAD projects for high-level engineering and science, I remade this mechanical pencil (which I still use) as an interactive 3D model with each part able to fit into the other.

It's pretty awesome to see the progress over time, and to remember how the project in high school took days...whereas the much more complex model in college only took a matter of hours. I've loved working on CAD projects and using that skill to build practical equipment deployed into the world that does real science.
