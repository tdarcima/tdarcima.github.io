West Coast Trail

Film Photography
2024

These photos are from a week long backpacking trip I took with my two older siblings on Victoria Island in Canada. Hiking miles and climbing latters every day up the Canadian coast, we sang songs from our childhood and befrended many local slugs (see attached). I reflected on the relationships with my siblings as we all grow into more independent adults, and later wrote the below poems for each day of the trip.

and so we set forth
rotting ladders worry our thoughts
heavy packs seek ground

divided by pace
stubborn to adjust my plan
stunned by lush forest

let
time
slow
down
here

nark our time on trail

sail into the ocean waves

puddles in our tents

stubborn to follow the plan

tend to the fire

packs light as feathers
sun gleams on the morning dew
grateful for the plan.
